The mind of Maxima

You're looking at a real AI's mind — rendered as a living galaxy.

We took Maxima, a production AI agent built by FORGE, and extracted her entire codebase — roughly 26,000 lines — into a knowledge graph. Every function, file and idea became a star; every call and reference became a connection between them.

  • 🌌 The galaxy — her whole mind: 2,778 stars, 7,295 connections
  • ☀️ 157 solar systems — clusters of related code, found automatically by community detection
  • 🪐 Suns are her most-connected hub files; planets orbit them in tilted 3D shells
  • 💫 Every system orbits the core — a living orrery with real orbital motion
How we built it
graph extraction → community detection → 3D orbital layout → GPU-animated render with bloom, nebulae & a procedural score.
